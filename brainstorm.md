## Pairwise vs the judge brief

- #3 Gary vs #5 drain-sock: Gary has the P-N49 inventory + named twist, but
  "mug in charge" is less grounded in the fault than the sock literally
  becoming the pump. #5 wins on honesty (criterion 3).
- #4 soup vs #9 oven-pizza: both have sharp number contrasts; soup's
  "unionised / negotiations at noon" is a bigger, instant surprise than
  "pizza trusts the smoke alarm". #4 wins on laugh (criterion 2).
- #7 boiler vs #1 fridge-milk: boiler's "Tuesday was 3 weeks ago" is a
  sharper surprise; fridge's "I am still running" restates beat 1 and wastes
  economy. #7 wins.
- #4 soup vs #5 drain-sock: drain-sock's twist IS the fault (strongest
  criterion 3) and has a double-number structure (42/41, 13/14), but soup's
  71°C-vs-12°C contrast is sharper and its twist escalates twice
  (unionised → negotiations at noon). Soup wins narrowly on criteria 1–2.
- #4 soup vs #7 boiler: boiler's twist is a time gag, less tied to the
  fault; soup's twist grows directly out of the uneven-heating fault.
  #4 wins.
